# Evidence Table (Helitrons, SINEs, MITEs)

Date: 2026-02-27

| TE Category | Claim | Evidence Summary | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Helitrons | HelitronScanner uses a two-layer terminal-motif learning/scoring workflow (LCV) rather than protein-first detection. | Authors describe training/prediction layers, separate terminal motif modeling, and score-based end pairing. | Xiong et al. 2014, PNAS. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1410068111 |
| Helitrons | HelitronScanner confidence is score-threshold sensitive and can trade sensitivity for false positives. | Paper discusses LCV score thresholds and randomized-genome false positive estimation. | PNAS full text. https://www.pnas.org/doi/full/10.1073/pnas.1410068111 |
| Helitrons | HELIANO is autonomous-signal-aware and uses terminal pairing/statistical filtering in its workflow. | NAR 2024 and tool docs describe transposase-centric discovery followed by terminal filtering/parameterized calling. | Li et al. 2024. https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkae610 ; Wiki: https://github.com/Zhenlisme/HELIANO/wiki |
| Helitrons | HiTE includes a dedicated Helitron module but is architected as a TE-wide full-length annotation pipeline. | HiTE workflow and Methods describe class-spanning modules (LTR/TIR/Helitron/non-LTR) and a HiTE-Helitron stage within the broader pipeline. | Hu et al. 2024. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-49912-8 ; PMCID: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11219922/ |
| Helitrons | HELIANO has a larger parameter surface than older motif-centric tools. | Dedicated parameter documentation shows many tunables and dependency on setup choices. | HELIANO parameters. https://github.com/Zhenlisme/HELIANO/wiki/Parameters |
| SINEs | SINE-Finder is a motif-template approach based on SINE structural hallmarks (A/B boxes, spacing, poly(A/T), TSD windows). | Methods section explicitly gives motif patterns and configurable spacers/TSD windows in the script workflow. | Wenke et al. 2011. https://doi.org/10.1105/tpc.111.088682 ; PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3203444/ |
| SINEs | SINE-Finder can miss low-copy or motif-degenerate families without parameter relaxation. | Authors report parameter adjustments needed for some families and mention undetected low-copy/motif-diverged families. | PMC article discussion. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3203444/ |
| SINEs | SINE_Scan integrates hallmark transposition signals, copy number, and structural signals in one framework. | Abstract directly states integrated evidence model and large-scale prediction framing. | Mao & Wang 2017. https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btw718 ; PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28062442/ |
| SINEs | SINE_Scan reports high sensitivity/specificity across diverse genome sizes in authors’ benchmarks. | Paper summary reports evaluation across 19 plant/animal genomes from ~120 Mb to ~3.5 Gb. | PubMed abstract. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28062442/ |
| SINEs | AnnoSINE combines pHMM homology and structural de novo search with explicit false-positive filtering to improve SINE annotation quality in plants. | Authors describe multi-stage candidate expansion and filtering with reported sensitivity/accuracy near or above 90% in tested plant genomes. | Li et al. 2022. https://doi.org/10.1093/plphys/kiab524 ; PMCID: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8825457/ |
| SINEs | AnnoSINE_v2 extends SINE annotation beyond plants and improves efficiency for larger genomes. | Authors report added animal pHMM support, improved computational efficiency, and >20% higher F1 than existing tools on animal datasets. | Liao et al. 2024. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13100-024-00331-y ; PMCID: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11490119/ |
| MITEs | MITE-Hunter outputs family consensus sequences for downstream homology masking workflows. | Abstract states consensus family outputs intended for library-based tools (e.g., RepeatMasker). | Han & Wessler 2010. https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkq862 ; PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20880995/ |
| MITEs | MITE-Hunter was benchmarked against FINDMITE and MUST with improved accuracy in reported tests. | Abstract reports high recovery in rice and significantly fewer false positives than compared tools. | PubMed abstract. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20880995/ |
| MITEs | MITE Digger is designed to reduce redundant computation in genome-wide MITE discovery. | Authors explicitly frame algorithm around removing redundant family-member processing overhead. | Yang 2013. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2105-14-186 ; PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23758809/ |
| MITEs | MITE Digger reports favorable runtime and low benchmark error rates in rice-scale testing. | Abstract reports ~15-hour rice-genome run with low false positive/negative rates in their evaluation. | PubMed abstract. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23758809/ |
| Cross-category | Multi-signal evidence fusion is an effective detection strategy in modern TE tools. | SINE_Scan integrates structural hallmarks + transposition signals + copy-number evidence and reports strong benchmark performance. | Mao & Wang 2017. https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btw718 ; PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28062442/ |
| Cross-category | Protein-first plus terminal-statistics fusion supports robust Helitron detection design. | HELIANO combines autonomous transposase evidence with terminal pairing/statistical filtering in its calling workflow. | Li et al. 2024. https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkae610 |
| Cross-category | Coarse-to-fine boundary refinement is a strong pattern for improving full-length TE calls (including Helitrons). | HiTE explicitly stages candidate discovery then dynamic boundary adjustment/filtering, improving precision of intact family reconstruction. | Hu et al. 2024. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-49912-8 ; PMCID: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11219922/ |
| Cross-category | TE display sequencing is a complementary insertion-dynamics assay, not a replacement for de novo TE library discovery tools. | The method enriches TE extremities to call non-reference insertions with very high sensitivity (reported down to 1 in 250,000), multiplexes across TE families, and can pair with Nanopore for rapid insertion identification. | Vendrell-Mir et al. 2025. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-025-03512-x ; PMCID: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11887134/ ; PMID: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40050910/ |
| Cross-category | Earl Grey provides a fully automated TE annotation pipeline focused on reducing fragmented/overlapping annotations and improving accessibility. | Authors benchmark across simulated and empirical datasets and report robust annotation/classification performance with standardized outputs. | Baril et al. 2024. https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msae068 ; PMCID: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11003543/ |
| Cross-category | Earl Grey, EDTA, and RepeatMasker are best treated as one whole-genome TE annotation/masking bucket for broad annotation context and benchmarking. | Earl Grey and EDTA are integrated end-to-end annotation workflows, while RepeatMasker is the masking/annotation engine frequently used in whole-genome TE workflows and evaluation. | Baril et al. 2024. https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msae068 ; Ou et al. 2019. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-019-1905-y ; RepeatMasker documentation/paper context (e.g., Smit et al.). |
| Cross-category | Protocol-driven multi-strategy TE/repeat workflows improve reproducibility and quality control in repeat-rich genomes. | The maize protocol lays out four end-to-end annotation strategies, with explicit quality-evaluation guidance and visualization scripts. | Benson et al. 2025. https://doi.org/10.1101/pdb.prot108578 ; PMID: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39237454/ |
| Cross-category | Explicit confidence scoring with threshold tuning is literature-backed for TE calls. | HelitronScanner uses LCV score thresholds and randomized-genome false-positive estimation to manage sensitivity/specificity tradeoffs. | Xiong et al. 2014. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1410068111 ; Full text: https://www.pnas.org/doi/full/10.1073/pnas.1410068111 |
| Cross-category | Efficiency-aware filtering/scoring is important for practical genome-scale discovery. | MITE Digger emphasizes redundancy-aware computation with reported low error rates in benchmarked datasets. | Yang 2013. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2105-14-186 ; PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23758809/ |

## Notes
- This table is evidence-linked and scoped to author-described methods and benchmarks, not independent re-benchmarking.
- Where claims involve "limitations," they are framed as method-intrinsic tradeoffs inferred from the published design and evaluation context.
- Cross-category source registry: `cross_category_sources.md`.

## Translation to RepBox v1.1 design
- `hybrid_score`: supported by cross-paper use of fused evidence channels (motif/structure + copy/similarity + contextual filters).
- `confidence_tier`: supported by score-threshold and calibration concepts used in published tools (e.g., HelitronScanner threshold/FPR handling).
- `evidence_flags`: supported by transparent, feature-level evidence reporting patterns in method papers (terminal motifs, copy support, boundary consistency, statistical filters).
